121. BEST TIME TO BUY AND SELL STOCK

Solution-

class Solution(object):
    def maxProfit(self, prices):
         maxProfit=0
         for i in range(len(prices)):
             for j in range(i+1,len(prices)):
                     profit = prices[j] - prices[i]
                
                     if profit > maxProfit:
                         maxProfit = profit

         return maxProfit

APPROACH- loops of i & j to point and compare 2 prices, and store (then compare again until maxProfit)
